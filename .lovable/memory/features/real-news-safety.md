name: Real news policy

---
- ALL real news is shown — wars, politics, conflicts included. No content filtering.
- News is simplified into calm, simple language appropriate for kids ages 5-15.
- No sides taken, no graphic language, but events are explained honestly.
- Never invent or fabricate stories. Every story must trace to a real BBC RSS article.
- Empty state shown if no articles are available — never generate fake fallbacks.
